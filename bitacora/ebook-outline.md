# Ebook Outline — "From Class Project to Startup: I Built a Real App with AI (and I'll Show You How)"

> Product to sell via TikTok ("comment X and I'll send you the link"). Fed by `diario.md`.
> The diary is raw and chronological (kept in Spanish, internal use); THIS ebook is the
> polished product and is written **100% in English — no Spanish anywhere**.
> Working title — iterate.

## Value proposition
By the end, the reader knows how to go from an idea to a real, published web app using AI
as a copilot, without being an expert. It is not "learn to code"; it is "learn to direct
the building of a product."

## Target audience
- Students and founders with an idea but no clear starting point.
- People who heard "with AI anyone can build apps now" and want to see it for real.

## Chapter structure (aligned to the TikTok episodes)

### Part 1 — Before writing a single line of code
- **Ch 1. Find a problem worth solving** (Ep. 1)
  - The real story: calculating my GPA in Excel took hours.
  - How to tell if your idea solves a real pain.
- **Ch 2. Validate before you build** (Ep. 2)
  - Ask 5-10 people. Forms, WhatsApp.
- **Ch 3. Define your MVP** (Ep. 3)
  - The discipline of "only 3 features" and leaving the rest for later.
  - What is in and what is NOT (and why subscriptions do NOT come first).

### Part 2 — Building with AI
- **Ch 4. Set the stage: give the AI rules and a plan** (Ep. 3-4)
  - Steering, specs, tasks. Why AI performs better with a written plan.
  - Plan mode vs Execution mode.
- **Ch 5. The skeleton: make it work ugly before pretty** (Ep. 4)
  - Minimal UI on purpose. Separating backend from frontend.
  - 🔥 "Real problem" box: the security vulnerability and the ESLint migration.
- **Ch 6. Where your data lives** (Ep. 5-6)
  - The database as "an Excel that lives on the internet, with rules."
  - 🔒 Box: publishable key vs secret key (security from day one).
- **Ch 7. The rules nobody can break** (Ep. 6)
  - Validations in the database, not just in the app.
- **Ch 8. The front door: accounts and security** (Ep. 7)
  - Login, Google, and "how each person sees only their own data."

### Part 3 — Launch and grow
- **Ch 9. Publish it for free** (Ep. 7-plan)
  - GitHub → Vercel → live public link.
- **Ch 10. Brand and online presence** (Ep. 8)
  - Name, logo, social profiles.
- **Ch 11. Make it official** (Ep. 9)
  - Business registration and tax ID (Colombia: Cámara de Comercio, RUT/DIAN).
- **Ch 12. Launch and get your first users** (Ep. 10)
  - University groups, collecting feedback, iterating.

## Recurring elements (what makes the ebook worth paying for)
- 🔥 **"Real problem" boxes:** every genuine stumble and how it was solved. This is what
  separates this ebook from a generic tutorial.
- 💬 **The exact prompt:** show, in plain language, what was asked of the AI and what it
  produced. (Prompts were originally in Spanish; translate/caption them in English.)
- ✅ **End-of-chapter checklists:** so the reader does the same with THEIR idea.
- 📸 **Screenshots:** of the moment something works (especially "the GPA calculates itself").
